# The Modelling Framework

### Linking AA → Collagen → ALP → Pi → Mineral

- The system couples **three biochemical modules**:
  - **AA-driven collagen production**
  - **ALP/β-GP–driven phosphate release**
  - **Ca–Pi supersaturation → ACP → HA mineral**
- Each module is:
  - minimal
  - mechanistic
  - independently identifiable from endpoints
- Goal: explain **basal vs osteogenic** outcomes using only **endpoints**, not time-series.

---

# Module 1 — AA → Collagen

- AA pulses at each medium change.
- Between pulses AA decays exponentially:

$$
C(t) = C_0 e^{-kt},
\qquad
t_{1/2} = \frac{\ln 2}{k} \approx 3\,\mathrm{h}.
$$

- Only the first 12 h/day contribute to hydroxylation.
- Daily collagen production:

$$
R(d) = R_0 + R_{\mathrm{AA}} A(d),
$$

where \(A(d)\) is the normalized AA exposure.

- Identifiable because we have **two day-14 collagen values** (basal & osteo).

---

# Module 2 — ALP + β-GP → Pi

- Total ALP:

$$
\mathrm{ALP}_{\text{tot}}(t)
= \mathrm{ALP}_{\text{serum}} + \mathrm{ALP}_{\text{cell}}(t).
$$

- Cellular ALP rises approximately linearly from day 3 → measurement day.

- Catalytic capacity per well:

$$
v_{\mathrm{ALP}}(t)
= 0.06\,\mathrm{ALP}_{\text{tot}}(t)
\quad [\mathrm{mM\,h^{-1}}],
$$

using

$$
1~\mathrm{mU} = 1~\mathrm{nmol/min}
= 60~\mathrm{nmol/h}
= 0.06~\mathrm{mM/h}
\quad\text{in a 1 mL well}.
$$

- β-GP substrate limitation:

$$
\frac{d\mathrm{Pi}}{dt}
= \min\bigl(v_{\mathrm{ALP}}(t),\,v_{\mathrm{BGP}}(t)\bigr),
$$

where \(v_{\mathrm{BGP}}(t)\) is the **maximal allowable rate** given remaining β-GP.

- β-GP resets at medium changes; Pi accumulates between.

- No free parameters: fixed by ALP endpoints and β-GP dose.

---

# Module 3 — Ca–Pi → ACP → HA

- Supersaturation:

$$
S(t) = \frac{[\mathrm{Ca}]^3[\mathrm{Pi}]^2}{K_{\mathrm{sp,eff}}}.
$$

- Collagen-gated ACP nucleation:

$$
r_{\mathrm{ACP}}(t)
= K_{\mathrm{nuc}}\,
\mathrm{Coll}(d(t))\,
\max\bigl(0,\,S(t) - S_{\mathrm{thresh}}\bigr)^n.
$$

- ACP → HA conversion:

$$
r_{\mathrm{HA}}(t) = K_{\mathrm{HA}}\,\mathrm{ACP}(t).
$$

- Unknowns: \(K_{\mathrm{nuc}}, K_{\mathrm{HA}}\), fitted from basal vs osteo ARS.

---

- Sobol global sensitivity analysis.
- Three modules studied independently:

1. **AA–collagen**  
   \(\mathrm{AA}_0,\ t_{1/2},\) medium interval, \(R_0,\ R_{\mathrm{AA}}\)

2. **ALP/β-GP → Pi**  
   ALP levels, β-GP dose, medium interval, ALP timing

3. **Mineral**  
   \(K_{\mathrm{nuc}}, K_{\mathrm{HA}},\) collagen scaling, Pi scaling, supersaturation parameters

- Outputs at days 7/14/21/28:

$$
\text{Collagen},\quad
\mathrm{Pi},\quad
\mathrm{HA},\quad
\mathrm{HA}_{\text{osteo}} - \mathrm{HA}_{\text{basal}}.
$$

- S1 (main effect) & ST (total effect) quantify dominance.

# Q1 — “AA decay function only captures decay, not supplementation.”

- The implementation **includes both**.

**Pulse at each medium change**

$$
C_{\mathrm{AA}}(t_i^+) = \mathrm{AA}_0.
$$

**Exponential decay between pulses**

$$
C_{\mathrm{AA}}(t) =
C_{\mathrm{AA}}(t_i^+) e^{-k(t-t_i)},\qquad
t_i < t < t_{i+1}.
$$

**Piecewise definition**

$$
C_{\mathrm{AA}}(t) =
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{AA}_0, & t = t_i,\\[4pt]
C_{\mathrm{AA}}(t_i^+) e^{-k(t-t_i)}, & t_i < t < t_{i+1}.
\end{cases}
$$

**Effective AA exposure (first 12 h of day \(d\))**

$$
E(d)=\frac{1}{12}
\int_{0}^{12} C_{\mathrm{AA}}\bigl(t + 24(d-1)\bigr)\,dt.
$$

---

# Q2 — “Why include \(R_0\) if AA is essential for collagen?”

- AA is essential for **maturation**, not **zero secretion**.
- Cells still secrete unhydroxylated procollagen; proliferation adds background.
- \(R_0\) captures:
  - basal immature collagen,
  - cell-number increase,
  - non-collagen IF baseline.
- Without \(R_0\), basal collagen would be forced to zero.

---

# Q3 — “Define \(A(d)\) and \(R_{\max}\) more clearly.”

**Normalized AA exposure**

$$
A(d)=
\frac{
\frac{1}{12}\displaystyle\int_0^{12}
C_{\mathrm{AA}}\bigl(t+24(d-1)\bigr)\,dt
}{
\displaystyle\max_{d'}
\left[
\frac{1}{12}\int_0^{12} C_{\mathrm{AA}}\bigl(t+24(d'-1)\bigr)\,dt
\right]
}.
$$

**Optional Michaelis–Menten collagen model**

$$
R(d)=R_0+
\frac{R_{\max} A(d)}{K_m + A(d)}.
$$

**Default linear model**

$$
R(d)=R_0 + R_{\mathrm{AA}} A(d).
$$

# Q4 — “Are you sure each module is identifiable?”

**AA → Collagen**

- Unknown parameters: \(R_0,\ R_{\mathrm{AA}}\)  
- Data: collagen at day 14 in basal and osteogenic conditions  
  \(C_{\text{coll,basal}}(14),\ C_{\text{coll,osteo}}(14)\)  
- ⇒ Two independent equations → unique solution for \((R_0, R_{\mathrm{AA}})\).

**ALP → Pi**

- No free kinetic parameters.  
- ALP\((t)\) is constrained by endpoint ALP measurements plus smoothness/positivity assumptions.  
- Once ALP(t) and β-GP dosing are fixed, \(\mathrm{Pi}(t)\) is structurally determined.  

**Mineral module**

- Unknown parameters: \(K_{\mathrm{nuc}}, K_{\mathrm{HA}}\).  
- Data: ARS at day 28 in basal and osteogenic conditions  
  \(\mathrm{ARS}_{\text{basal}}(28),\ \mathrm{ARS}_{\text{osteo}}(28)\).  
- Monotonic model ⇒ unique pair \((\log K_{\mathrm{nuc}}, \log K_{\mathrm{HA}})\) that matches both endpoints.

**Lumped quantities**

- Cell counts, collagen quality, and matrix maturity are **not** fitted separately; they are intentionally **lumped** into effective parameters.

---

# Q5 — “Normalize by cell number? Per-cell for ABM?”

- No measured cell counts at day 14 or during mineralization.
- \(R_0\) and \(R_{\mathrm{AA}}\) are therefore **per-well effective outputs**:

$$
\text{Collagen signal} \propto
(\text{collagen per cell}) \times (\text{cell number}).
$$

**For ABM coupling**

1. Choose or simulate a plausible proliferation curve \(N(d)\).
2. Convert per-well collagen to per-cell:

$$
\text{collagen per cell}(d)
=
\frac{\text{collagen per well}(d)}{N(d)}.
$$

3. Apply analogous scaling to Pi inputs if needed.

- This keeps the ABM consistent with the continuum model **without inventing unmeasured cell counts**.

# Q7 — “Do fitted parameters make biological sense?”

- AA half-life \(t_{1/2} \approx 2{-}6\,\mathrm{h}\): consistent with AA instability in α-MEM.
- ALP→Pi conversion:

$$
1\,\mathrm{mU} = 1\,\mathrm{nmol/min}
= 60\,\mathrm{nmol/h}
= 0.06\,\mathrm{mM/h},
$$

matching the 0.06 factor in \(v_{\mathrm{ALP}}(t)\).

- Mineral kinetics: \(K_{\mathrm{nuc}}, K_{\mathrm{HA}}\) give ACP → HA dynamics on hours–weeks timescales, consistent with in-vitro mineralization.

---

# Q8 — “Why identical Pi accumulation despite different ALP shapes?”

- β-GP is the limiting substrate:

$$
\frac{d\mathrm{Pi}}{dt}
= \min\bigl(0.06\,\mathrm{ALP}(t),\,v_{\mathrm{BGP}}(t)\bigr).
$$

- When

$$
0.06\,\mathrm{ALP}(t) \gg v_{\mathrm{BGP}}(t),
$$

the system is **substrate-limited**.

- Integrated Pi:

$$
\int \frac{d\mathrm{Pi}}{dt}\,dt
$$

depends mainly on β-GP resets, not ALP shape → nearly identical cumulative Pi curves.

---

# Q9 — “Basal: no supersaturation → no nucleation?”

- Basal Pi low, collagen low, Ca constant.
- Supersaturation:

$$
S(t)=\frac{[\mathrm{Ca}]^3[\mathrm{Pi}]^2}{K_{\mathrm{sp,eff}}}
$$

never exceeds \(S_{\mathrm{thresh}}\).

Thus:

$$
r_{\mathrm{ACP}}(t)=0
\;\Rightarrow\;
\mathrm{ACP}(t)=0
\;\Rightarrow\;
\mathrm{HA}(t)=0,
$$

matching flat basal ARS.

- symbol

- sensitivity range  
- literature vs fitted

**Clarified mineral interpretation**

- Collagen **enables** nucleation (template).
- Pi + Ca **drive** supersaturation via \(S(t)\).
- \(K_{\mathrm{nuc}}\): onset and rate of ACP formation once \(S > S_{\mathrm{thresh}}\).
- \(K_{\mathrm{HA}}\): conversion rate from ACP to HA.
- Together control onset, slope, and plateau of HA accumulation in basal vs osteogenic conditions.
