# **1. Yang et al., 2025 — Biomaterials Advances (IF ~6)**

### *Topological defects induce heterogeneity in MSC osteogenesis*

**Cells:** hMSCs
**Core idea:** MSC monolayers spontaneously form **+1/2 and –1/2 topological defects**.

**Findings:**

* Defect centers drive **centripetal & centrifugal** cell migration patterns.
* **Defect geometry → local proliferation & osteogenic heterogeneity**.
* Mechanical stresses at defect cores likely activate **RUNX2 / SOX9**.
* Demonstrates that **collective nematic organization directly regulates fate**.

**Why it matters for us:**

> First direct link between **nematic defects** and **osteogenic differentiation**.

# **2. Xie et al., 2024 — Cellulose (IF ~6.3)**

### *LC viscoelastic matrices regulate YAP & osteogenesis*

**Cells:** rBMSCs
**Core idea:** Liquid-crystal (LC) hydrogels impose **viscoelastic directional cues**.

**Findings:**

* LC substrates enhance **cytoskeletal alignment** and stress fiber formation.
* Strong, stable **YAP nuclear localization** on LC matrix.
* Upregulation of **ALP, OPN, mineralization**.
* LC mechanical anisotropy mimics native bone ECM.

**Why it matters:**

> Shows how **LC ordering + mechanics** translate to transcriptional activation.

# **3. Liu et al., 2022 — Composites Part B (IF ~13)**

### *LC-mimetic ECM gels enhance MC3T3-E1 osteogenesis*

**Cells:** **MC3T3-E1**
**Core idea:** Chitin-based LC gel replicates bone ECM’s natural liquid-crystalline structure.

**Findings:**

* Enhanced **adhesion, spreading, proliferation** of MC3T3s.
* Increased **Ca–P mineralization** and osteogenic activity.
* Viscoelastic LC microenvironment stabilizes cell orientation.
* Links LC ordering → **matrix deposition**.

**Why it matters:**

> Direct evidence for LC-driven osteogenesis in **your exact cell model**.

# **4. Zhang et al., 2025 — Chemical Engineering Journal (IF ~15)**

### *Microgrooved LC templates guide polarization & osteogenesis*

**Cells:** BMSCs
**Core idea:** LC hydrogels with microgrooves = **osteoid-like topological architecture**.

**Findings:**

* LC state + grooved topology increase **RGD exposure** & protein adsorption.
* Drives **cell alignment & polarization**.
* Strong activation of **Rho/ROCK mechanotransduction**.
* Boosts osteogenic markers and mineralization.

**Why it matters:**

> Demonstrates synergy between **topology + LC ordering** in controlling fate.

# **5. Hileuskaya et al., 2023 — Colloids & Surfaces A (IF ~6.2)**

### *MSCs behave as active nematic systems on patterned substrates*

**Cells:** MSCs
**Core idea:** Treat MSC monolayers as **active nematic phases**.

**Findings:**

* Ridge/groove surfaces generate **isotropic → nematic transitions**.
* Orientation measured with **P₂ order parameter** (liquid-crystal metric).
* Alignment strengthens with **flagellin-modified surfaces**.
* Contact guidance directly linked to nematic physics.

**Why it matters:**

> Gives the **quantitative nematic tools** needed for MC3T3/hMSC orientation analysis.
