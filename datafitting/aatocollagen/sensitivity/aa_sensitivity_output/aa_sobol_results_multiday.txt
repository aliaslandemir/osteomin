
Global Sobol sensitivity of the AA → collagen module
----------------------------------------------------

Parameter ranges:
- AA0: 30.0–70.0 µg/mL
- half-life: 2.0–6.0 h
- medium interval: 1.0–3.0 days
- R0_factor: 0.5–1.5
- R_AA_factor: 0.5–1.5

Sobol settings:
- Base N = 1000, used N = 1024 (power of 2)
- Total evaluations = 12288
- Collagen evaluated at days [7, 14, 21, 28]

Key findings (example based on day 28):
- medium_interval_days is dominant (ST ≈ 0.519)
- R0_factor and R_AA_factor contribute moderately (ST ≈ 0.224, 0.303)
- AA0 and half-life show negligible influence (ST ≈ 0)

Across days 7→28, the relative importance of the
medium-change interval and the collagen production rates is conserved,
reflecting cumulative collagen deposition driven mainly by dosing
schedule and effective per-day synthesis, rather than uncertainties in
AA dose or decay kinetics.
